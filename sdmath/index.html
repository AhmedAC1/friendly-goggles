AC's Math Reps

Splitting division

\[ \frac{244}{2}=\begin{bmatrix} \frac{200}{2}=100\\ \frac{40}{2}=20\\ \frac{4}{2}=2 \end{bmatrix}\to100+20+2=122 \]

Splitting multiplication

\[ 563\times2=\begin{bmatrix} 500\times2=1000\\ 60\times2=120\\ 3\times2=6 \end{bmatrix}\to1000+120+6=1126 \]

Norms (Euclidean norm for vectors)

\[ \sqrt{5^2 + 6^2} \]

Excluded numbers

\[ 6 = \cancel{6}\to5 \] \[ 65 = \cancel{65}\to64 \] \[ 10 = \cancel{10}\to9 \] \[ 5 \text{ to } 10 = \cancel{5 \text{ to } 10}\to5 \text{ to } 9 \]

Powers

\[ 10^6=10\times10\times10\times10\times10\times10 \]

Logarithms (reverse)

\[ \log_{10}(1,000,000) = 6 \]

Summation and products/factorials

\[ \sum^5_{i=1}i = 1+2+3+4+5 \] \[ \prod^5_{i=1}i=5\times4\times3\times2\times1 \] \[ 5! = 1\times2\times3\times4\times5 \]

Integrals (for constant bounds)

\[ \int^5_0x\,dx=\frac{5^2}{2}-\frac{0^2}{2}=12.5 \]

Delta

\[ \Delta x = x_\text{final} - x_\text{initial} \]

Example

\[ r = \text{rating of show} \] \[ \Delta r = 9-8 = 1 \]

Reciprocals

\[ \frac{3}{4}\to\frac{4}{3} \] \[ \frac{3}{4}\times\frac{4}{3}=1 \text{ (always for non-zeros)} \]

Limits

\[ \lim_{x\to10}5x = 5(10)\to50 \] \[ \lim_{i\to5}10 \times i=10\times5\to50 \]

Imaginary numbers

\[ i^1=i \]\[ i^2=-1 \]\[ i^3=-i \]\[ i^4=1 \]\[ \text{repeats every } 4 \text{ powers} \]

Number sets

\[ \large\begin{matrix} \mathbb{Z} = \{\ldots, -1, 0, 1, \ldots\}\\ \mathbb{R} = \{-3,0,\frac{1}{2},2,\sqrt{2},\pi,\ldots\}\\ \mathbb{N} = \{1, 2, \ldots\}\\ \mathbb{Q} = \{\frac{1}{2},3,-4\} \end{matrix} \] \[ \begin{matrix} \forall x,\qquad x\in\mathbb{R}\\ \forall x\in\mathbb{Z},\qquad x\times1=x \end{matrix} \]

Plus

\[ 5+1=(5\uparrow)\to6 \] \[ 5+2=(5\uparrow\uparrow)\to7 \]

Subtraction

\[ 5-1=(5\downarrow)\to4 \] \[ 5-2=(5\downarrow\downarrow)\to3 \]

Multiplication

\[ 5\times1=5 \] \[ 5\times2=5+5 \]

Division

\[ \frac{5}{1}=(1\times\underline{5})=5 \] \[ \frac{5}{2}=(2\times\underline{2.5}=5)=2.5 \]

Powers alternative: Knuth's up-arrow notation

\[ 2\!\uparrow\!4=2^4=16 \] \[ 3\!\uparrow\!\uparrow\!2=3^3=27 \] \[ 3\!\uparrow\!\uparrow\!\uparrow\!4=3\!\uparrow\!\uparrow\!(3\!\uparrow\!\uparrow\!3) \]
